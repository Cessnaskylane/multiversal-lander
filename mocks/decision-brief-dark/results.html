Should I refinance my mortgage at 5.8%?

Download PDF
Bottom line

Probably yes, if your current rate is 6.8% or higher and you’ll stay at least 4 more years. At a smaller gap, closing costs usually wipe out the savings.

72%
Confidence
5 of 6 models agree

Where the models agree

  • Work out your break-even: closing costs ÷ monthly savings
  • A rate drop of about 1 point is the usual threshold
  • Avoid resetting to a new 30-year term unless you need cash flow

Where they disagree

  • Grok: wait 6 months; rates may fall further
  • Claude: rate forecasts are unreliable; decide on today’s numbers
  • Mistral: a 0.5-point drop can be enough on a large balance

What could change the answer

  • Closing costs above 3% of the loan
  • Selling or moving within 3 years
  • A lower credit score than when you bought

Next steps

  1. Find your current rate and remaining balance
  2. Get Loan Estimates from 3 lenders on the same day
  3. Compare break-even month against how long you’ll stay
