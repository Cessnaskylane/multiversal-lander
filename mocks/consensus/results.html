Is intermittent fasting better than calorie counting for fat loss?

Ask a follow-up
Consensus answer

Not in general. When total calories and protein are matched, intermittent fasting and calorie counting produce similar fat loss. Fasting mainly works by making it easier for some people to eat less. Pick whichever you can stick with; adherence matters more than the method.
