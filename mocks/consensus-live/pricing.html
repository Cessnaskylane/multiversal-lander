What counts as an ask?

One question sent to your selected models, including the Consensus answer. Follow-ups count as new asks.

How is the confidence score calculated?

It reflects how closely the models’ answers match on the key claims. It measures agreement, not guaranteed accuracy.

Is there a free plan?

No. Every ask costs real model fees, so plans are paid from day one. You can cancel anytime.

Are you affiliated with these AI companies?

No. We access each model through its public API and show answers as they come back.
