Is intermittent fasting better than calorie counting for fat loss?

Ask a follow-up
Consensus answer

Not in general. When calories and protein are matched, intermittent fasting and calorie counting give similar fat loss. Fasting mainly helps some people eat less. Pick the one you can stick with.
