Is a Decision Brief professional advice?

No. It’s a structured summary of what several AIs say. Use it to prepare for a doctor, lawyer or financial adviser, not to replace them.

How is confidence calculated?

It reflects how closely the models agree on the bottom line and key facts. It measures agreement, not guaranteed accuracy.

Is my question private?

Your questions are sent to each model provider to get answers. We don’t sell your data, and you can delete any brief.

Are you affiliated with these AI companies?

No. We access each model through its public API.
