# Multiversal Glass Runway — 10 strong variations — 2026-09-13

Base DNA: pale ice / frosted glass / translucent panels / ultra-light typography / quiet couture.  
Not neon sci-fi, not Magma rainbow, not dark cyber.

Each folder: `look.html` + `page-desktop.png` (1280×800). Phone optional.

---

## 01 — Original Plus
Refined classic frosted 2-column panel (cleaner spacing, lighter type, softer crop) — control baseline.

## 02 — Full-bleed frost
Entire viewport is one frosted sheet over a soft photo; centered type floats; no side-panel split.

## 03 — Vertical ribbon
Tall narrow frosted column left on ice field; full-height portrait occupies the opposite field.

## 04 — Stacked glass cards
Three overlapping translucent cards (name / promise / CTA) with portrait peeking behind the stack.

## 05 — Runway strip
Ultra-wide thin frosted bar across mid-screen; huge spaced MULTIVERSAL above; face fades below the bar.

## 06 — Mirror panes
Two frosted panes side by side (voice list | open portrait) with a hairline divider like storefront glass.

## 07 — Mist hero
Soft gradient mist, almost no panel border; giant light wordmark; photo as atmospheric wash not hard crop.

## 08 — Floating island
Single rounded frosted island floating upper-third; empty ice field below with micro nav.

## 09 — Casting glass
Frosted header bar + four translucent portrait tiles in a row — agency board through glass.

## 10 — Editorial frost
Left half ice typography (serif + light sans); right half frosted photo frame with thick white margin.
