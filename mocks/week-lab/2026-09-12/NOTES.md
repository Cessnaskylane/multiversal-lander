# Week lab · 2026-09-12 — lander look mocks

First-pass interactive HTML alternatives to the live multiversal.ai lander (dark bokeh + centered portrait door flow).

## Look A — Door corridor + YouTube stage
`look-a-door-corridor.html`

Why it beats live:
- Spatial corridor of named doors creates place and choice hierarchy; live feels like a single centered card on bokeh.
- Opening one door drives a cinematic portrait panel — product affordance, not moodboard collage.
- YouTube `@multiversalbots` is a persistent stage strip (avatar + mini surface + CTA), equal to product chrome, not footer spam.
- Sparse HUD + thin pink rail keeps the calm/cold brand without decorative orb noise.

## Look B — Liquid type / gravity lens
`look-b-liquid-lens.html`

Why it beats live:
- Magma wordmark is the hero with CSS liquid distortion on hover — brand mark does work instead of sitting above bokeh.
- Doors become gravity-lens orbs with clear hover/active mass; selection flyout + dock make it feel product-real.
- YouTube is a glowing portal tile in the same grid as voice orbs — first-class peer, not an afterthought.
- Promise line stays front-and-center; no “explore infinite possibilities” fluff.
