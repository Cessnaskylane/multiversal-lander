# Multiversal hip directions — 2026-09-13

Art direction for Andy: maison / model-board / celebrity personal brand energy.  
Not techy, not sci-fi, not neon cyber, not Magma rainbow MULTI, not bokeh orbs.

Each folder: `logo.png` (+ `logo-1x1.png`), `page-desktop.png` (1280×800 @2x), `page-phone.png` (390×844 @2x), `look.html`.

---

## 01 — Ivory Maison
Cream paper field, black Bodoni/Didot wordmark **MULTIVERSAL** + tiny `.ai`.  
Huge serif headline + one full-bleed fashion portrait; quiet “Put on Grok.” Couture stillness.

## 02 — Night Board
Elite/IMG casting board: pure black/white, asymmetric face grid, thin condensed Syne mark top-left.  
Agency energy, no pink glow — celebrities as the product surface.

## 03 — Soft Studio
Warm taupe + chalk cards; lowercase soft sans **multiversal**.  
Rounded casting-deck portraits — personalities you flip through like talent.

## 04 — Hot Press
Vogue-cover stack: cream + lacquer red, massive **MULTI / VERSAL**, single cover face, date-line microcopy.  
Magazine authority without SaaS chrome.

## 05 — Glass Runway
Pale ice gray, frosted translucent panel, ultra-light spaced caps.  
Almost invisible UI — runway quiet, not dashboard.

## 06 — Sunset Cast
Amber/rose dusk wash over cinematic crop; cream serif on photo.  
Celebrity casting-call vibe at golden hour.

## 07 — Mono Zine
Off-white Xerox press: stamped bold type, dashed rules, B&W torn-edge portrait.  
Underground fashion cooler — skips the neon on purpose.

## 08 — Pastel Agency
Soft lilac → mint sidebar, geometric **M.** monogram + wordmark.  
Comme/SSENSE-adjacent pastel agency calm — not babyish.

## 09 — Gold Quiet
Espresso velvet + champagne foil wordmark; one portrait.  
Quiet wealth, not crypto gold.

## 10 — Italic Gossip
Instrument Serif italic logo only on white; sparse name list like a party invite.  
Social/celeb RSVP energy — not a product page.
