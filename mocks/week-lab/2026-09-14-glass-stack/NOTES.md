## Why it should beat live Glass on iPhone

Live `multiversal.ai` Glass (and `2026-09-13-glass-flow`) is an editorial desktop split: frost portrait right, wrapping chip corridors left. On 390px that collapses into mid-screen chip soup — labels wrap/overflow, tap targets shrink below thumb comfort, face fights the chip field for hierarchy.

Glass Stack fixes that with layout, not lipstick:

1. **Face owns the top (~50–58%)** in a frosted frame. Choices never compete mid-viewport with the portrait.
2. **Vertical frosted ROWS** (min ~52px tap height) instead of wrapping pill chips. No horizontal overflow on 390px. Scroll the stack; one column only.
3. **One primary Continue in the thumb zone** (52px CTA). Back is secondary. Gates unchanged: no model / no verses → soft hint.
4. **`@multiversalbots` is a phone-native channel ROW** with YouTube avatar — peer to X/TikTok/IG — not a fat desktop stage strip.
5. **Self-improve vs `2026-09-12-phone`:** keeps reel/dossier/split lessons (face-first hierarchy, big rows, one CTA) while wearing live Glass DNA so it can actually replace the Glass runway on mobile without abandoning pale ice.

Night-depth boards under `2026-09-14-p2-depth/` were optional soft influence only; this deliverable is a full product click-through, not a BG board.
